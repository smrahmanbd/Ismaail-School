How big is a mole?

A mole is just a counting word — like "dozen" — but for a number so huge it only makes sense for atoms.

The idea in one line

1 mole = 6.02 × 1023 things

A pair is 2. A dozen is 12. A ream is 500. A mole is 602 000 000 000 000 000 000 000. That number is called Avogadro's number. Chemists need it because atoms are far too small to count one by one.
