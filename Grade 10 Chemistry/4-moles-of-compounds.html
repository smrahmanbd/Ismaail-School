Moles of compounds: a formula is a recipe

The little numbers (subscripts) tell you how many of each atom — per molecule and per mole.

The idea

H₂O means "2 H for every 1 O". That's true for one molecule, a dozen molecules, or a mole of molecules. So 1 mol of H₂O contains 2 mol of H atoms and 1 mol of O atoms.

Molar mass of a compound = add up (subscript × atomic mass) for every element. Brackets multiply everything inside: Ca(OH)₂ = 1 Ca + 2 O + 2 H.
