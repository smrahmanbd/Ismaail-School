Percent composition: slices of the mass pie

What fraction of a compound's mass comes from each element?

The idea

If a 400 g pizza has 100 g of cheese, cheese is 100 ÷ 400 × 100 = 25 % of the pizza by mass. Same for elements:

% element = mass of element in 1 molmolar mass of compound × 100

Trap: in water, hydrogen is 2 of the 3 atoms (67 %), but only about 11 % of the mass. Percent composition is always about grams. Toggle the two pies to see it.
