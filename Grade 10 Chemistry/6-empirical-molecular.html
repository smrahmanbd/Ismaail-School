Empirical & molecular formulas

From lab data to the simplest ratio — and then to the real molecule.

The idea

Empirical formula = the simplest whole-number ratio of atoms (like simplifying 6/12 to 1/2). Glucose C₆H₁₂O₆ simplifies to CH₂O.

Molecular formula = the real count in one molecule = (empirical formula) × n, where n = molar mass ÷ empirical-formula mass. n is always a whole number.
