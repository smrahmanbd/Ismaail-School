# Grade 10 Chemistry — The Mole (interactive)

Animations, virtual experiments and practice problems for **Module 9: The Mole** (Lessons 1–5).
Open `index.html` in any web browser — no installation needed. Works on phones, tablets and computers.

| # | Page | What you can do |
|---|------|-----------------|
| 1 | [How big is a mole?](1-how-big-is-a-mole.html) | Egg cartons vs moles, a ladder of huge numbers, "could you count a mole?", representative particles |
| 2 | [Particles ↔ moles](2-particles-and-moles.html) | Pick-the-right-fraction game with animated unit cancelling, science-notation helper |
| 3 | [Molar mass & the Mole Map](3-mole-map.html) | A balance comparing 1 mol of two elements, an interactive grams ⇄ moles ⇄ particles map |
| 4 | [Moles of compounds](4-moles-of-compounds.html) | Build any formula and watch its molar mass add up, mole-ratio slider, grams → atoms ladder |

| 6 | [Empirical & molecular formulas](6-empirical-molecular.html) | Step-by-step formula machine with snapping bars, the "shrinking oven" for finding n |

## How to view
- **On a computer:** download the folder (green **Code** button → *Download ZIP*), unzip it, and double-click `index.html`.
- **As a website:** make the repository public, then turn on **Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → main / root**. The pages will be at `https://<username>.github.io/Ismaail-School/Grade%2010%20Chemistry/`.

## Notes
- All pages are plain HTML + JavaScript (no libraries, no internet needed once downloaded).
- Atomic masses are rounded the way most Grade 10 textbooks round them (H 1.008, C 12.01, O 16.00, …), so answers may differ from the book in the last digit.
- They go with the beginner study guide *The Mole — Study Guide* (PDF).
