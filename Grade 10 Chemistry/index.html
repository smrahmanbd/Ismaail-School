Animations, virtual experiments and endless practice for Module 9 (Lessons 1–5).

How to use this

Go in order. On each page: read the short idea box, play with the animation until you can predict what it will do, then try the check questions. Finish with Practice until you can get 10 in a row. You need nothing else — no installs, works on a phone, tablet or computer.

The one picture to remember: MASS ⇄ MOLES ⇄ PARTICLES. Moles are always the bridge.
